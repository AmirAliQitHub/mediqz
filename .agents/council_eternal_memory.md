## 3. IDENTIFIED DEFECT IN AUTO ROUTINE SORTER
- **Current Defect:**
  1. Destructive Overwrite: Calling `handleApply` wipes out all existing items across all days in `useRoutineStore`, destroying schedules previously built for other courses.
  2. Part Truncation & Math Flaw: Parts are iterated sequentially up to `durationDays * partsPerDay`. If a course has 15 parts and user sets 7 days @ 2 parts/day, only 14 parts are scheduled and the 15th part is silently dropped! Conversely, if a course has 6 parts and 7 days are created, the last day is an empty dead zone.
  3. No Merge/Append Choice: Students cannot choose whether to "جایگزینی کل برنامه" (Replace All) or "افزودن و چینش در روزهای موجود" (Append / Distribute into existing days).
  4. Missing Course Part Telemetry: Student cannot see how many parts exist in the chosen course or how many days are mathematically required before confirming.
