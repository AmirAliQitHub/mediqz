## 2. SYNCHRONIZED AUTONOMOUS STEP LIFECYCLE

1. **PHASE 1 — Pre-Execution Scope Lock:**

2. **PHASE 2 — Isolated Worker Execution (Zero Batching):**

   - Only upon formal `COUNCIL_APPROVAL` from the Gatekeeper:
   - Update `task.md` to mark `[x]` for that single step.
   - Advance to Phase 1 of the next unchecked `[ ]` step (unless a user-commanded Hard Stop is active).
